Good to know
  • Entry requires Commander Lv. 45; prep-stage points do not carry into the main event.
  • Military Support actions only count inside Eternal Land; daily top-1000 also earn overall-leaderboard points (500 → 20 for ranks 1 → 1000).
  • Chapter completions unlock the next building tier even if the goal isn't met.
  • Ark participation counts toward the "Participate in N Ark fights" tasks (2 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 40). Some arks' first battle may be Jul 24.
  • Special Resource Areas are the Amethyst source (fortress/power-station currency) — 10 areas across three waves.
  • Server transfers are locked for the duration (Jul 19 – Aug 8).
