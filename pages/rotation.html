Alliance Rotations

Each week a few players are invited to spend some time visiting another alliance, a chance to fight alongside new people and see how another camp runs things. It's an invitation, not an assignment: if the timing is wrong for you, just tell your alliance leadership and it's a no-hard-feelings pass. Players who declined or didn't show aren't listed here.
