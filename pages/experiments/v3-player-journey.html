Step 4

Run the numbers before you commit

Three live calculators

These pull straight from the decoded game tables. No estimates — the cost ladders match the patch data exactly.

How many Awakening Stones do I need to upgrade a skill?

Per-skill ladder. Pulls cost 15 / 15 / 30 / 30 / 45 / 45 / 60 / 80 / 130 stones across Lv. 1–10 (5 pulls per level). Lv. 10 is free.

All 4 skills to Lv. 10 on one hero costs 9,000 stones. All 6 Phase I heroes fully maxed = 54,000 stones.

How many Hero Shards + ES Skill chips do I need for the 40-level ladder?

Awakening level (not skill level). Each hero needs their own personal Hero Shard + ES Skill chip. The ladder runs Lv. 0 → 40 and adds +2,000 Fight Power per level.

Full 0 → 40 costs 1,140 Hero Shards + 740 ES Skill chips (per hero) and adds +80,000 Fight Power.

Should I swap my donor, or keep training them?

Hero Swap converts 100% of your donor's upgrade materials into the target's shards + ES chips. Donor's 10 unlock-shards stay on the donor. Talents two-way swap. Swap itself is free.
