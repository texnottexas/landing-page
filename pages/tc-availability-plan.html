Why we field five squads

Four of them belong to DOG and MSS, sitting on the three battle times most of the roster signed up for. DGen takes the fifth. The -6 slot stays on the board as somewhere to put the few players who cannot make anything else, which is a different job from fielding a squad. Across every arrangement we tested, the number of squads big enough to take buildings came out the same.
