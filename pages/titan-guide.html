How points work

Scoring rules

Two tracks run at once: your alliance's Camp score decides the win; your Individual score decides your personal rewards. Verbatim from the in-game "How to Play".

Camp / Alliance

  • Occupying facilities
  • Crystal Cluster Labs
  • Hover Stations
  • Acquiring Bounty Points

Individual

  • Everything on the left, plus…
  • Killing rival units
  • Occupation / Gathering / Bounty / Kill scores all roll up here

Occupation & full control

Attack a building to occupy it, then hold it until its countdown ends to gain full control. A fully-controlled building adds Camp score every minute. Countdown = 5 min for perimeter buildings, 15 min for Nexus / Gateway, 20 min for the Hover Station Ark.

Bounty Points

Held buildings accumulate Bounty over time. When a rival takes a building, its stored Bounty scatters around it - and anyone can scoop it up for Camp + Individual score. Bounty also charges your Commander skill meter.

Hover Station scoring

The Ark opens ~15:00 and runs 3 cycles of 5 rounds. Each round randomly matches you against another camp; at each cycle the four camps are ranked 1st to 4th and earn Camp + Individual score by placement. Deploy one troop, arrive before a battle stage to join, and withdraw anytime. It is also the #2 tie-breaker below, so in a close match it decides the winner.

Holding a Repository

Fill the grid, deny the teleport

A captured Discarded Repository sits in the middle of an 8×8 zone. Park bases and towers on every open cell and the enemy has nowhere to teleport in - they'd have to fight through the wall. The footprint is fixed (12 big bases on the outer ring, 12 cells hugging the building); the only question is how many slots are towers (from Mechanical Masters, up to 5 each) versus bases.

  • Building (2×2) - dead-centre, can't be parked on.
  • Big base (2×2) - one player. The outer ring holds exactly 12.
  • Small base (1×1) - one player in a single cell.
  • Tower (1×1) - a Mechanical Master builds up to 5 (2 strength, 2 weakening, 1 urgent care). The inner ring holds 12 1×1 cells.
Repository Big base (2×2) Small base (1×1) Tower (1×1)

Short on Mechanical Masters? Swap, don't leave gaps.

The only thing that seals the building is a full grid - an empty cell is a teleport-in door. Towers are stronger, but a base denies the teleport just as well.

1
Towers protect the core first. Spend MMs on the inner ring before anywhere else - it is the last line before the capture point.
2
Out of MMs? Drop a base in that cell. Every tower cell can be a 1×1 small base instead - still sealed.
3
Short on bodies? Big bases are most efficient - one player covers a 2×2. Keep the outer ring as 12 big bases.
4
Minimum to seal it: 24 slots - 12 big bases + 12 inner units. Make as many inner units towers as your MMs allow.
