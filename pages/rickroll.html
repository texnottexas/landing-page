Mobile setup
  1. Add this page as a bookmark.
  2. Copy the Ops Center link below.
  3. Go to your bookmarks and edit that bookmark.
  4. Edit the address, hit paste to replace it, and give it a memorable name.
  5. Open the game in your browser, then run the bookmark:
    • iPhone (Safari): in Settings → Apps → Safari → Tabs, pick Bottom or Top (Compact won't run bookmarks like these). Open the game, tap the book icon, then tap the bookmark. Firefox works too.
    • Any desktop browser: just click the bookmark.
    • Android (Chrome): tap the address bar, type the bookmark's name, then tap the suggestion that appears. Tapping it from the bookmarks list will not run it.

First time playing in a browser?

The Ops Center runs in the game in a web browser, not in the app. These steps get your main account into a browser.

  1. 1

    Pick a browser

    We recommend Firefox. Safari and Chrome work too, but their bookmarks take extra setup.

    Firefox for iPhoneApp Store Firefox for AndroidGoogle Play

    On a computer, any browser works. Once it's installed, open this page (2864tw.com/rickroll) in that browser too. You'll need it in step 7.

  2. 2

    Check how you sign in

    In the game app, tap your profile picture, then Account.

    Look at Google and Facebook. The one that's lit up, with your name under it, is how you sign in. Remember it for step 6.

  3. 3

    Open the game in that browser

    In the browser from step 1, go to this address:

    h5.topwargame.com/h5game
    then paste it into the address bar
  4. 4

    Play the tutorial until you cut the wire

    The browser starts you on a new guest account. Play the tutorial until you cut the wire. That's far enough.

  5. 5

    Open the login screen

    Tap your profile picture, then Settings at the bottom left.

    Then tap Register/Log in.

  6. 6

    Log in and switch to your main account

    Tap Login with Facebook or Login with Google, whichever was lit up in step 2. Then switch to your main account on Server 2864.

  7. 7

    Set up the Ops Center bookmark

    Watch the video for your browser and do what it shows. It starts with the Ops Center link above.
